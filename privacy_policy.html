Privacy Policy

Google Play Store Compliance Document for Offline Android Applications

Effective Date: September 14, 2026
Scope: Covers all offline utility, calculator, and productivity applications published on the Google Play Store under developer publishing accounts.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of mobile applications developed and published on the Google Play Store ("Applications"). Our suite of applications is engineered to function entirely offline on your local device. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting any data associated with your device, while ensuring compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google AdMob/AdSense requirements, and applicable data protection laws.

2. Core Philosophy: 100% Offline Architecture

All applications covered under this policy are designed specifically as offline utilities. This means:

  • No Personal Data Collection by Developer: We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information, user credentials, device identifiers, or usage telemetry on our own servers.
  • Local Data Processing: All application operations, calculations, inputs, and generated files occur locally within your device's isolated application storage sandbox.
  • No Account Required: Users are never required to register, sign up, or provide an email address or phone number to use the core features of our applications.

3. Data Collection by Third-Party Advertising Partners (Google AdMob & AdSense)

Although our applications operate completely offline in terms of core functionality, advertisements may be displayed within certain free-tier versions of our apps to support ongoing maintenance and development. These advertisements are served by Google LLC through Google AdMob and Google AdSense frameworks.

When an active internet connection is present on your device, third-party advertising SDKs integrated into the app may automatically collect certain non-personal or technical information to serve contextual or personalized advertisements. This collection is performed independently by Google on our behalf as an ad network provider.

3.1 Data Collected Automatically by Google Advertising SDKs

Google AdMob / AdSense may automatically collect and process data including, but not limited to:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / AAID) and device-level hardware identifiers.
  • Network & Connection Information: IP address, network connection type (Wi-Fi, mobile data), and mobile network operator.
  • App Interaction & Performance Data: Ad interaction metrics, ad impression data, click logs, crash logs, and general diagnostics required for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate Location: Derived coarse location based on IP address routing.

Important Note on Offline State: When your device is completely offline (disconnected from Wi-Fi and mobile data), advertisements cannot load, and no ad-related data transmission or collection occurs during that time.

4. How Google Uses Advertising Data

Google utilizes the data collected through its SDKs to:

  • Deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements based on user preferences and settings.
  • Measure ad performance, evaluate ad campaign effectiveness, and calculate billing for advertisers.
  • Detect, prevent, and mitigate fraudulent ad clicks, spam, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce Google's publisher policies.

For more detailed information regarding how Google collects, uses, and shares data when you use our apps, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms regarding partner sites.

5. User Choices & Ad Personalization Controls

You maintain full control over your privacy settings and ad personalization preferences:

  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising by modifying your device settings. On Android devices, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads and toggle on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or reset your Advertising ID.
  • Consent Management Platforms (CMP): In regions requiring explicit consent (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA), our apps present a compliant consent dialog allowing you to manage your cookie and advertising consent preferences.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided data through third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures with Google AdMob child-directed settings (TFCD / TFUA).

7. Data Security

Because our applications operate locally on your device and we do not maintain centralized cloud databases or servers storing user data, your personal files and inputs remain secure on your device hardware. Data transmitted to Google AdMob/AdSense is protected via industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption during transit.
